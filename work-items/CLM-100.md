# CLM-100 - Show the estimated settlement on a claim

**As a** claims handler **I want** to see the estimated settlement on a claim **so that** I can tell the customer what to
expect before the claim is assessed.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1** The claim detail response (`GET /api/claims/{claimId}`) includes `estimatedSettlement` in GBP with 2 decimal places.
- **AC2** The estimate is the claimed amount minus the excess that applies to the claim.
- **AC3** The calculation is covered by automated tests.

## Notes

- Business rules: claims rulebook (the `claims-rules` skill).
- Out of scope: showing the estimate in the web app.
